## T-INT-1 — Cluster gate item (Technical vs. non-Technical lean)

A friend's bike chain keeps slipping off halfway through your ride together.

**A)** You get off, turn the bike over, and work through the gears one at a time until you find exactly where it's slipping. *(Technical/Investigative lean)*

**B)** You suggest walking it to the nearest shop and asking someone there to take a look. *(Social/Enterprising lean — not scored within this cluster)*

## T-INT-2 — Informatique-SI ("build") vs. Sécurité-SI ("defend")

You're helping run sign-ups for a small event.

**A)** You spend your time improving the sign-up form so it's smoother and can handle more people at once. *(Informatique-SI lean)*

**B)** You spend your time watching for suspicious duplicate entries or people trying to grab extra spots. *(Sécurité-SI lean)*

## T-INT-3 — Électronique ("hardware") vs. Informatique-SI/Sécurité-SI ("software/abstract")

A remote-control car stops responding properly — sometimes it turns when you're not touching the controller.

**A)** You open it up and check the wiring and connections, testing each part with your hands. *(Électronique lean)*

**B)** You look up whether it's a known issue with the control signal and try reconfiguring it. *(Informatique-SI/Sécurité-SI lean)*

## T-INT-4 — Informatique-SI's coordination/leadership lean vs. independent deep-focus

Your group project has several moving parts due at the same time (design, writing, presentation).

**A)** You take charge of tracking what's done, what's late, and reassigning tasks so nothing falls through. *(Informatique-SI lean)* **[Extraversion]**

**B)** You'd rather stay focused on your one piece and get it as good as possible, letting someone else handle the overall plan. *(Independent deep-focus lean)* **[Introversion / low Extraversion]**

## T-APT-1 — General systematic-diagnosis instinct (cluster-wide)

The wifi at home suddenly stops working for everyone.

**A)** You check the router lights, then test one device at a time to see if it's a single-device problem or a whole-network problem. ***(correct instinct)*** **[Conscientiousness]**

**B)** You restart everything at once and hope it works.

**C)** You wait for it to fix itself, or ask someone else to deal with it.

## T-APT-2 — Sécurité-SI vigilance instinct

You're added to a group chat you don't recognize, and it starts asking members to click a link to "confirm their spot" in an event.

**A)** You look closely at the link and how the message is phrased before doing anything — something about the pattern feels off. ***(correct instinct — Sécurité-SI)***

**B)** You click the link, since it's probably fine — most messages like this are.

**C)** You mention it to whoever runs the group and let them decide what to do about it.

## T-APT-3 — Électronique diagnostic instinct

A ceiling lamp at home starts flickering.

**A)** You check the bulb, then the socket, then the switch, one at a time, in that order. ***(correct instinct — Électronique)*** **[Conscientiousness]**

**B)** You start poking at whatever's easiest to reach first, to see if that fixes it.

**C)** You ask someone who deals with electrical stuff a lot to take a look, since it's their thing.

## T-APT-4 — Informatique-SI systems/edge-case instinct

You're building something (a form, a schedule, a small app) for a group of 30+ people to use.

**A)** Before finishing, you think through what happens if two people try to do the same thing at once, or what happens with unusual inputs. ***(correct instinct — Informatique-SI)***

**B)** You build the straightforward version first and only deal with edge cases if someone actually runs into one.

**C)** You focus mainly on making it look good, since most people won't hit an edge case anyway.

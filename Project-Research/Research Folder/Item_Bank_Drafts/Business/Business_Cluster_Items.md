## B-INT-1 — Cluster gate item

A local bakery near your neighborhood is struggling to attract customers.

**A)** You start thinking of ways to promote it and grow its customer base — social media, deals, events. *(Business/Enterprising lean)*

**B)** You ask the owner what's actually going wrong day-to-day and try to help fix that specific problem directly. *(Technical/practical lean — not scored within this cluster)*

---

## B-INT-2 — E-commerce vs. Économie-Gestion

You're put in charge of your class's fundraiser.

**A)** You focus on designing eye-catching posts and a fun online campaign to get people excited. *(E-commerce lean)* **[Openness]**

**B)** You focus on setting a budget, tracking who's paid, and making sure the numbers add up at the end. *(Économie-Gestion lean)* **[Conscientiousness]**

---

## B-INT-3 — E-commerce leadership/dynamic-team lean vs. Économie-Gestion structured-planning lean

Your fundraiser idea grows bigger than expected and now needs a small team to run it.

**A)** You enjoy dividing up roles, keeping people motivated, and adjusting the plan as things change day to day. *(E-commerce lean)* **[Openness]**

**B)** You'd rather map out a clear plan beforehand that the team just follows, minimizing surprises. *(Économie-Gestion lean)* **[Conscientiousness]**

## B-APT-1 — General proactive-opportunity instinct

You notice a lot of people at school complaining about the same small inconvenience (e.g., a long printer line).

**A)** You start thinking about how you could actually fix or improve this for people. ***(correct instinct)***

**B)** You agree it's annoying but don't think further about it.

**C)** You mention it to a teacher and let them decide if it's worth fixing.

---

## B-APT-2 — E-commerce customer/creative instinct

You're designing a flyer for a school event.

**A)** You imagine how someone scrolling past would react in the first two seconds, and design around grabbing their attention. ***(correct instinct — E-commerce)*** **[Openness]**

**B)** You focus on making sure all the necessary information (date, time, location) is clearly and correctly included.

**C)** You leave the design to someone else and focus on other tasks.

---

## B-APT-3 — Économie-Gestion quantitative/analytical instinct

You're deciding whether to keep paying for a club activity.

**A)** You work out roughly what it's costing you each month vs. what you're getting out of it before deciding. ***(correct instinct — Économie-Gestion)*** **[Conscientiousness]**

**B)** You go with how you've been feeling about it lately — enjoying it or not.

**C)** You ask a friend already in the club what they think you should do.

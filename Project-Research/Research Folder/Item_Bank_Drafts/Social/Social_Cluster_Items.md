## S-INT-1 — Cluster gate item

A friend has been quiet and withdrawn for the past few weeks.

**A)** You make time to sit with them one-on-one and gently ask what's going on beneath the surface. *(Social/Helping lean)*

**B)** You suggest a plan to get them out and busy — signing up for a club or activity together. *(Enterprising lean — not scored within this cluster)*

---

## S-INT-2 — Psychologie Clinique vs. Orientation et conseil

You're asked to help a younger student who's been struggling this year.

**A)** You want to understand what's really going on for them personally — spend real time getting to the root of it, one-on-one. *(Psychologie Clinique lean)* **[Openness]**

**B)** You look at their overall situation — classes, teachers, workload — and think about what changes to their environment or schedule would help. *(Orientation et conseil lean)* **[Extraversion]**

---

## S-INT-3 — Depth-with-individuals vs. pattern-spotting-across-many

Over a semester, you keep being the person classmates come to for advice.

**A)** You find yourself drawn to the deep, complicated cases — the ones with a lot going on underneath. *(Psychologie Clinique lean)* **[Openness]**

**B)** You find yourself naturally spotting patterns — noticing several people running into the same kind of problem and thinking about a general fix. *(Orientation et conseil lean)* **[Extraversion]**

## S-APT-1 — General attentiveness-to-incongruent-cues instinct

A friend is telling you about a problem, but their words and their tone don't quite match.

**A)** You notice the mismatch and gently ask what's really going on, rather than just responding to their words. ***(correct instinct)*** **[Agreeableness]**

**B)** You take what they say at face value and respond to that directly.

**C)** You wait to see if they bring it up again on their own before saying anything.

## S-APT-2 — Psychologie Clinique root-cause depth (option C fixed)

Someone tells you they've been having trouble sleeping lately.

**A)** You find yourself wondering what's underneath it — stress, something unsaid, a bigger pattern. ***(correct instinct — Psychologie Clinique)*** **[Openness]**

**B)** You think of practical tips they could try tonight to sleep better.

**C)** You assume it's probably temporary and don't read much into it yet.

## S-APT-3 — Orientation et conseil systemic pattern recognition

Three different students mention feeling overwhelmed by the same class this term.

**A)** You start wondering if it's something structural — pacing, workload, timing — rather than about each student individually. ***(correct instinct — Orientation et conseil)*** **[Extraversion]**

**B)** You focus on helping each of the three work through it individually, one at a time. *(Psychologie Clinique lean, legitimate different specialty fit)*

**C)** You wait to see if more students mention it before deciding it's worth looking into.

### Existing internal tool check: "Pathfinder" (pathfinder.his.edu.dz)
Checked to avoid duplicate work. **Confirmed: not an orientation/matching test.** It's a general student services portal (Student Affairs, Academic Advising, Finance, Library, IT Support, club directory, admin forms). It does have a section literally called "Orientation," but it's described as helping *already-enrolled* first-year students adapt to university life — not a pre-enrollment specialty-matching tool. **No overlap risk**, but Pathfinder's existing "Orientation" section is a natural future home for this test once built, given it's already the portal students land on. Worth coordinating with whoever maintains Pathfinder early rather than bolting on later.

## 1. Starting Point: Analysis of Jobmi.fr (benchmark competitor)

Jobmi is a French orientation platform for 18–25 year-olds using a RIASEC-based quiz (30 questions, 5-point Likert scale, ~6-8 min).

**Strengths identified:**
- RIASEC is a legitimate, established framework (Holland's model).
- Honest framing — repeatedly tells users the test gives "pistes, pas une réponse figée" (leads, not a fixed answer), not a verdict.
- Pushes users toward real-world verification after the quiz (internships, job-shadowing) rather than stopping at the quiz result — the right instinct.

**Weaknesses identified:**
1. **Low item count per dimension** — 30 questions ÷ 6 RIASEC dimensions ≈ 5 items/dimension, meaning a single ambiguous question or bad mood can swing results noticeably.
2. **No visible reverse-scored/consistency-check items** — no way to catch someone answering incoherently.
3. **No published validation** — no evidence the test was piloted or correlated with real outcomes.
4. **Likert scale invites response-style bias** — enthusiastic people rate everything high, reserved people rate everything low, so you partly measure personality-of-answering rather than actual traits.
5. Business model is lead-gen for their internship/immersion marketplace — not inherently bad, but worth being aware the quiz's job is partly to capture an email.

**Conclusion:** Jobmi is a fine *entry point* but not a diagnostic tool. Useful benchmark, not a template to copy as-is.
